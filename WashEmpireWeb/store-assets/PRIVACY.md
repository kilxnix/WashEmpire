# Wash Empire — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: July 21, 2026_

Wash Empire is a premium (paid) game. We built it to work without collecting your data.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Wash Empire does not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal information. The game has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no network features.

## Where your game data lives

Your save game (progress, settings, and sound preference) is stored **only on your device**, in the app's local storage. It never leaves your device unless you explicitly use the in-game "Save backup" feature to copy it yourself.

If you uninstall the app, your local save is deleted by the operating system. We cannot recover it — use the in-game backup feature if you want to keep it.

## Third parties

The paid version of Wash Empire contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs and makes no network requests during play.

## Children

Wash Empire does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If a future version of the game changes any of the above (for example, a free ad-supported edition), this policy will be updated and the change will be clearly noted in the store listing.
