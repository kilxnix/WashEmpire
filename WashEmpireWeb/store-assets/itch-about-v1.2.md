# Wash Empire — Car Wash Tycoon

Build a self-serve car wash from one rough corner lot into a five-district empire. Cars pull in, fill the pay box with bills, coins, and tokens, and you collect every dollar by hand — then pour it straight back into better bays, more staff, and new districts across the city.

## The loop

Cars decide whether to pull in based on demand, traffic, bay condition, and open capacity. Every wash drops a mix of bills, coins, and tokens in the pay box — collect it, count the week, and reinvest. Miss too many and they drive right past.

## Features

- Watch every upgrade appear on the lot — wands, foam curtains, dryer arches, signage
- Run marketing campaigns — flyer runs, TV spots, and Wash Weekend events that visibly drive traffic
- Hire a cash runner, bay technician, and night manager to automate the grind
- Buy and restore five districts, each with its own traffic and pricing
- Ride through your own automatic conveyor tunnel in first-person Ride View
- Offline earnings — the empire keeps washing while you're away
- Earn the three Crowns — Empire, Pristine, and Wealth — and become a Legend
- Legacy runs: restart with a permanent earnings bonus and climb again
- **Pay once. No ads, no in-app purchases, no data collection.**

## How to play

Name your location and hit **Open Wash**. Use **1× / 3× / 10×** to control time, **Collect** when the pay box fills, **Upgrades** to improve bays and hire staff, and **Map** to buy and restore new districts. Tip: set **Graphics** to **Balanced** or **High** to see the full visuals — **Low** keeps it fast on older devices.
