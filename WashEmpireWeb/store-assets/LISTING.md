## Full description

Run a self-serve car wash from one rough corner lot to a five-district empire.

Cars pull in, feed the pay box with bills, coins, and tokens — and you collect every dollar by hand. Upgrade each bay's equipment, hire staff to run the week for you, restore run-down lots until the whole block shows up, and expand from Rustwater Junction to a fully automatic conveyor tunnel on the city beltline.

FEATURES
• Watch every upgrade appear on the lot — wands, foam curtains, dryer arches, signage
• Collect the pay box and count the take, week by week
• Run marketing campaigns: flyer runs, TV spots, and Wash Weekend events that visibly drive traffic
• Hire a cash runner, bay technician, and night manager to automate the grind
• Buy and restore five districts, each with its own traffic and pricing
• Ride through your own automatic wash tunnel in first-person Ride View
• Offline earnings while you're away — the empire keeps washing
• Premium: pay once, no ads, no in-app purchases, no data collection

Complete the empire. Then push the weekly take as high as it can go.
