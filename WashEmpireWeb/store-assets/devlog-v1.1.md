# Wash Empire v1.1 — Same Lot, New Shine

When the first build of Wash Empire went out, the loop was all there: cars roll in, the pay box fills, you collect, you upgrade, you expand across five districts chasing three crowns. It was a game. It just... looked like a prototype — flat boxes under flat light.

So v1.1 is a top-to-bottom visual overhaul. No new mechanics, no changes to the numbers. Just the lot finally looking like a place you'd actually want to run. Here's what went into it.

## New light

The whole scene got real lighting: image-based reflections so car paint, glass, and water actually shine, a warm key light, and a soft bloom on the sunlit surfaces and the lit signage. This one change did the heaviest lifting — the diorama went from "flat" to "has depth" almost overnight.

## The wash bays, rebuilt

The bays used to be plain boxes with thin roof rails. Now they've got **curved canopy arches, rounded wall caps, and a pillared entrance**. And a row of marquee lamps runs across the top — they light up one at a time as you buy each piece of bay equipment. Buying an upgrade should *feel* like something changed on the lot, and now it does.

## Six kinds of car

Every customer used to be the exact same box. Now they roll in as **sedans, hatchbacks, SUVs, pickups, vans, and sports cars** — rounded roofs, hubcaps, and a brighter mix of paint. A queue finally looks like traffic instead of a stack of clones.

## A wash that moves

The wash itself was mostly a puff of mist. Now there are **spinning scrub brushes and a foam curtain** during the soap phase, **pulsing rinse jets**, and a little **gold sparkle** as each car rolls out clean.

## Curb appeal

A tall roadside **pylon sign** with a glowing water-drop logo, plus shrubs, flower planters, and trees around the lot. It reads like a destination now, not a slab of concrete.

## Five moods

Each district got its own atmosphere — warm and bright in the small town, dusky neon downtown, icy and luminous up in the snow. Same lot, five very different feelings, all from the sky, fog, and light.

## ...and cars that stop driving through each other

With all that new traffic, cars would occasionally clip straight through one another. So we taught them some manners: cars in the wash hold their spot, arriving cars yield to them, drive-bys yield to everyone, and the whole line keeps a respectful gap. (It's purely visual — your revenue is exactly the same to the penny.)

## The endgame gets the glow-up too

The automatic conveyor tunnel — the district you grind *toward* — was still wearing the old flat look while everything else got remade. Couldn't leave the finish line as the ugliest part of the map. It now carries the same canopy arches and a grand branded entrance arch, so the reward looks as good as the starting lot.

## One fun fact

None of this is sprites or imported 3D models. The entire lot — every bay, car, brush, arch, and shrub — is built out of plain shapes in code. Which made this update equal parts art pass and geometry homework.
