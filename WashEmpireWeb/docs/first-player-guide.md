# Wash Empire First Player Guide

Wash Empire is a browser car-wash tycoon. Your job is to keep cars moving, collect the pay boxes, improve each bay, hire help, and expand from a small corner wash into higher-traffic districts.

## Quick Start

1. Name your location and press `Open Wash`.
2. Let cars enter the bays and wash. Use `1x`, `3x`, or `10x` to control time.
3. Watch the pay box. When cash is waiting, press `Collect`.
4. Open `Upgrades` and buy early bay upgrades. Bay upgrades are usually the fastest way to improve the first few minutes.
5. Use the `Map` once you can afford new districts or restoration work.
6. At the end of each week, review revenue, costs, wages, drive-bys, and pay-box cash, then open the next week.

## Main Controls

- `Pause`, `1x`, `3x`, `10x`: control simulation speed.
- `Upgrades`: buy lot upgrades, individual bay upgrades, and employees.
- `Map`: buy, restore, and switch city districts.
- `Graphics`: cycles `Low`, `Balanced`, and `High`. Use `Low` on slower devices and `High` for screenshots.
- `Watch Ad`: watches a rewarded-ad placement or local fallback and grants a timed 3x cash boost when completed.
- `Collect`: empties the physical pay boxes and pays any week-closeout costs that are due.
- `Ride View`: appears only in the automatic conveyor district.

## The Core Loop

Cars choose whether to enter based on demand, traffic, bay condition, and available capacity. Cars that cannot fit may queue or drive by. Drive-bys are missed sales, so rising queue pressure usually means you need more throughput, more appeal, or a better district strategy.

Each wash creates a mix of bills, coins, and tokens in the pay boxes. Some later upgrades and staff can reduce leakage or automate collection. If you do nothing, money waits in the boxes until you collect or staff handles the weekly closeout.

## Upgrades

Bay upgrades improve individual bays:

- `Selector Dial`: better instructions and faster setup.
- `High-Pressure Wand`: faster washes and less condition loss.
- `Foam Soap System`: better price and slower condition loss.
- `Spot-Free Rinse`: better price and demand.
- `Air Dryer Boom`: faster bay turnover.
- `Bill/Token Vault`: more token use and less cash leakage.

Lot upgrades improve the whole property:

- `Paint Job`, `New Signage`, `Vacuum Island`, `Security Lighting`, `Loyalty App`, and `Mobile Ad Campaign` increase demand.
- `Coin Box Cameras`, `Card Readers`, and employees reduce collection pain or leakage.
- `Touch-Free Laser Wash` is a late-game premium upgrade.

Most upgrades also add visible props to the scene, so the lot should look better as the run improves.

## Employees

Employees cost money up front and wages each week, but they reduce manual work:

- `Cash Runner`: collects weekly if you do not collect first.
- `Bay Technician`: collects weekly and restores bay condition.
- `Night Manager`: collects weekly, trims leakage, and increases demand.

Hiring too early can slow your cash growth, but staff is powerful once weekly revenue can cover wages.

## Cities

The starter district is `Rustwater Junction`. Later districts cost more but bring better traffic, pricing, patience, or special layouts:

- `Harbor Heights`: higher token use and solid traffic.
- `Neon Downtown`: tiny footprint, premium pricing, heavy traffic.
- `Frostpeak Resort Row`: patient travelers and high late-game value.
- `Beltline Express Tunnel`: automatic conveyor wash with ride view.

Newly purchased districts can be restored through the map. Restoration increases the district's readiness and helps the site feel less run-down.

## Weekly Review

A week is short in browser time. When the week ends, time pauses and the review panel shows:

- Revenue and profit.
- Fixed costs and employee wages.
- Cars served and drive-bys.
- Lost sales from missed customers.
- Auto-collected cash, if staff handled it.
- Physical pay-box cash still due.

Press the closeout button to pay costs, collect due cash, and open the next week.

## Saves And Offline Progress

The browser version autosaves to local browser storage. Keep using the same browser and device if you want to keep the same run.

When you return after being away for more than a minute, the offline return panel credits partial idle earnings. Active ad boost time is honored first, then normal offline earnings continue.

## First 10 Minute Tips

- Start at `3x` while learning. Use `10x` once the wash is stable.
- Collect early and often until you hire staff.
- Buy cheap bay upgrades before saving for the expensive lot upgrades.
- If drive-bys rise, improve throughput with wand/dryer upgrades or increase demand and patience with lot upgrades.
- Do not hire every employee immediately. Make sure weekly revenue can cover wages.
- Use `Balanced` graphics on most devices; switch to `Low` if the browser stutters.
